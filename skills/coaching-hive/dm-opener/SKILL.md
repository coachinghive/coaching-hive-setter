# DM opener

A good opener is about them, short, and ends with an easy question.

## Steps
1. Read `~/second-brain/voice.md` and the lead's profile, post or comment.
2. Write 3 options, each under 40 words:
   - one referencing something specific they posted or said
   - one about the problem in `~/second-brain/ideal-client.md`
   - one light and friendly
3. No links, no price, no "I help people like you", no pitch.
4. Show the 3 options. The coach picks and sends it themselves.

## Never
- Send it. The coach or their VA sends every cold DM by hand.
- Message anyone who hasn't interacted with the coach first.
