# Objection reply

## Steps
1. Name the objection: price, time, trust, timing, partner, tried before, other.
2. Find the coach's answer in `~/second-brain/objections.md`.
3. Draft a reply: acknowledge, ask one question to understand, then the
   coach's answer, then a next step (usually a call).
4. If the objection isn't in the file, say so, draft your best reply marked
   `NOT IN SECOND BRAIN`, and ask the coach for their real answer to save.

## Never
- Offer a discount, payment plan or guarantee that isn't written in `~/second-brain/offer.md`.
