# Follow-up

## Steps
1. Read the thread. If the lead has replied since your last message, stop:
   draft a reply instead.
2. Draft by day:
   - **24h**: a short, specific question about their goal.
   - **48h**: something useful: a tip, a client win (no names), or a free resource from `~/second-brain/offer.md`.
   - **72h**: an easy out: "No worries if now's not the time. Want me to check back next month?"
3. Each under 35 words, in the coach's voice.
4. After 72h with no reply, mark the lead `nurture` and stop.

## Never
- More than 3 follow-ups. Never guilt, pressure or fake urgency.
