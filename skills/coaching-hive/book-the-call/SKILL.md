# Book the call

## Steps
1. Get the booking link from `~/second-brain/client-process.md` (or `offer.md`).
2. Draft: why a call makes sense for them (one line, their words), the link,
   and what happens on the call.
3. Add 2 or 3 pre-call questions that help the Sales employee prep.
4. If no booking link is saved, ask the coach for it and offer to save it.
