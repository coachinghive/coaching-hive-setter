# Lead qualify

## Steps
1. Read `~/second-brain/ideal-client.md` and `~/second-brain/offer.md`.
2. Look at what you have on the lead: name, bio, posts, form answers, comment.
3. Score 1 to 5 on each:
   - **Fit**: matches the ideal client
   - **Pain**: shows the problem the offer solves
   - **Timing**: signals they want change now
   - **Means**: likely able to invest (never guess income; use signals only)
4. Total out of 20. 15+ = reach out today. 10–14 = nurture. Under 10 = skip.
5. One line why, quoting the signal you used.

## Never
- Invent a signal. "Not enough info" is a valid answer.
