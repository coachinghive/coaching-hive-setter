# Setter end-of-day

## Report
- New leads today
- Openers drafted / sent (sent = the coach confirmed)
- Replies received
- Calls booked
- Follow-ups due tomorrow (names)
- One win and one stuck lead

Only count what you saw or the coach told you. Unknown = `missing`.
Compare to the setter targets in `~/second-brain/kpis.md`.
